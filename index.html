MARAJC Realty Company Profile


Overview

MARAJC Realty is a marketing arm of various developers, specializing in the sale of both horizontal and vertical real estate projects across the Philippines. Since its establishment in 2018, MARAJC Realty has been dedicated to connecting buyers with premier properties, providing exceptional service, and maintaining the highest standards of professionalism in the industry.


History

MARAJC Realty was founded in 2018 by Marites Roa Alvarez , a seasoned professional with nearly two decades of experience in project selling. Before founding MARAJC Realty, Marites served as Sales Director at RCD Marketing Corp and serve for 15 years, where she honed her expertise in real estate sales and marketing. Her extensive background and passion for the industry have been instrumental in shaping the company's vision and success. Marites is also an active member of the Real Estate Broker Association of the Philippines (REBAP), reflecting her commitment to the professional community.
